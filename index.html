The listener asksWhat are you seeking?

How to make machines understand languages that have little data, no settled spelling, and tones that change the meaning of a word.

Tonal speech recognition

Self-supervised speech models adapted to a new language, and the explicit modelling of lexical tone alongside the sounds.

Data as scientific infrastructure

Speech corpora at national scale, annotation quality, writing and tone normalisation, shared benchmarks.

Reliable voice interfaces

What a recogniser gets wrong, and which errors are unacceptable when the user cannot read the transcript.

Speech translation

From Fongbe speech to French text, with corpora and reference systems used in international evaluation campaigns.

Machines that read science

Question answering, relation extraction and claim verification over the research literature.

Evaluation of language models

Reference sets and model-based judgement for systems that retrieve, reason and write.

The listener asksWhat have your hands made?

  1. Today
    I lead the science of a national speech programme.Scientific lead for the collection and annotation of Beninese speech, tones included, and for the recognition models, benchmarks and voice services built on it. Associate researcher at the Université de Parakou, where I also teach and contribute to doctoral supervision.
  2. Since 2023
    I direct the technology of an AI company.Chief Technology Officer of Opscidia, in Paris. I set the technology vision and the research roadmap with the chief executive, decide where investment goes, recruit and lead a team of researchers and engineers, and answer to industrial clients for what is delivered.
  3. Since 2023
    I rebuilt a platform while it was in use.I directed its move from a single block to independent services, so that it could scale, hold under load and evolve without interruption.
  4. 2020 – 2023
    I carried research into production.As head of AI research and development, I led work on language models, information extraction, knowledge graphs and scientific fact-checking, and turned it into evidence-grounded question answering, agentic search and assisted report writing.
  5. 2017 – 2020
    I built machines that converse.Research scientist at CEA List, on dialogue and language understanding, including a virtual patient that lets future doctors practise the consultation.
  6. 2013 – 2016
    I taught a machine to hear Fongbe.Doctorate in computer engineering, automation and signal processing, between France and Benin. No system could transcribe continuous Fongbe speech; I collected the voices, modelled the sounds and the language, and built the first one.
